# Satchel 0.1.4

Choose whether text pastes with its formatting or as plain text by default.

## What's new

**Paste text as**

- Settings has a new **Paste text as** option: **Formatted text** (the default, as before) or **Plain text**.
- A normal paste uses the format you chose: `Return`, double-click, the Paste button, `⌘1` to `⌘9` and `⌘C`, also when you paste several clips at once.
- The second paste action (`⇧Return`, the second button, the second item in a clip's menu) uses the other format. Its label follows your choice: **Paste as Plain Text** when formatted text is the default, **Paste with Formatting** when plain text is.
- Files and images are not affected. Files paste as files, or as their paths with the second action.

## Good to know

- A plain-text paste carries no formatting, so apps such as TextEdit and Notes give it the style at the cursor. Pasted right after bold text, it comes out bold; that is the app's doing, the same as with macOS's own Paste and Match Style.

## Updating

- From 0.1.1 or later: Satchel offers this version itself. Look for the dot on the menu bar icon, or choose **Check for Updates…** from its menu.
- From 0.1.0: download the DMG below and drag Satchel to Applications, replacing the old one. Your history and settings stay as they are.

## Known limits

- macOS 11 or later on Apple silicon only. Opening at login needs macOS 13 or later.
- No sync between Macs.
