# Satchel 0.1.5

Deleting a clip no longer sends the shelf back to the start.

## Fixes

- **The shelf stays where it is when you delete.** Deleting a clip (`⌘⌫` or Delete in its menu) used to select the first clip and scroll the row back to the beginning. Now the next clip slides into the deleted clip's place and is selected. If you delete the last clip in the row, the one before it is selected.
- **The same when several clips are deleted at once,** and when clips are removed by the history limits while the shelf is open.

## Updating

- From 0.1.1 or later: Satchel offers this version itself. Look for the dot on the menu bar icon, or choose **Check for Updates…** from its menu.
- From 0.1.0: download the DMG below and drag Satchel to Applications, replacing the old one. Your history and settings stay as they are.

## Known limits

- macOS 11 or later on Apple silicon only. Opening at login needs macOS 13 or later.
- No sync between Macs.
