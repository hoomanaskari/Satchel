# Satchel 0.1.0

The first public version of Satchel: a fast, private clipboard manager for macOS 11 (Big Sur) or later.

## Getting started

1. Download the DMG below, open it and drag Satchel to Applications.
2. Open Satchel. It lives in the menu bar, not in the Dock.
3. Press `⌘⇧V` to open the shelf.
4. The first time you paste, Satchel asks for the Accessibility permission. Choose **Open Accessibility Settings…**, turn Satchel on, and paste again.

## What's in it

**Your clipboard history**

- Keeps text (with its formatting), links, code, colors, images and files copied in Finder.
- Paste as plain text whenever you like.
- Text up to 2 MiB and images up to 64 MB (100 megapixels) are kept. A bigger copy isn't kept, and the shelf tells you.

**The shelf**

- Slides up from the bottom of the screen, over the Dock, on the display under the pointer. Liquid Glass on macOS 26.
- Each card shows the app it came from, the kind of clip, when you copied it and a preview.
- Search as you type, and filter by Text, Links, Code, Images, Colors or Files.
- Keyboard first: arrows to choose, `Return` to paste, `⇧Return` for plain text, `⌘1`–`⌘9` for the first nine clips, `⌘C` to copy, `Space` for a larger preview, `⌘⌫` to delete, `Escape` to close.
- Select several clips with `⇧`- or `⌘`-click and paste them in order.
- Give a clip its own title.

**Pinboards**

- Pin clips (`⌘P`) to named, colored pinboards. Pinned clips are kept forever.

**Link previews**

- Link cards show the page's title and image.

**Settings and the menu bar**

- How many clips to keep (2,000 by default) and for how long (90 days by default).
- Change the shortcut, choose whether picking a clip pastes or only copies, and turn image capture or link previews on and off.
- Pause capturing from the menu bar or Settings.
- Clear the whole history.
- The menu bar icon plays a short animation each time a clip is saved (none with Reduce Motion on).

## Privacy

- Your history stays on your Mac, in a database only your user account can read. No accounts, no sync, no analytics, no update checks.
- Copies from password managers and other excluded apps, and copies marked concealed or temporary, are never kept. You choose the excluded apps in Settings.
- Deleted clips are overwritten, not just hidden.
- **Link previews are on by default and are the only thing that uses the network.** Satchel fetches each copied web link once, from that link's own site, so that site sees the link and your IP address. Sign-in, password-reset, one-time and private-network links are never fetched. Turn previews off in Settings > Shelf, and Satchel makes no network requests at all and deletes the saved previews.

## Known limits

- macOS only for now.
- Satchel doesn't update itself yet. Watch this repository's releases for new versions.
- No sync between Macs.
- PDF-only and RTFD-only copies aren't kept.
