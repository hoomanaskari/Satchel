# Satchel 0.1.2

A small release that confirms automatic updates work end to end.

## What's new

- Nothing changes in how Satchel works. This is the first version delivered through the built-in updater.

## Updating

- From 0.1.1: Satchel offers this version itself. Look for the dot on the menu bar icon, or choose **Check for Updates…** from its menu.
- From 0.1.0: download the DMG below and drag Satchel to Applications, replacing the old one. Your history and settings stay as they are.

## Known limits

- macOS 11 or later on Apple silicon only.
- No sync between Macs.
