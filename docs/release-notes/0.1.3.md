# Satchel 0.1.3

Satchel stays out of the Dock, opens at login, and opening it again shows the shelf.

## What's new

**Opens at login**

- On macOS 13 or later, Satchel now opens when you log in, quietly in the menu bar: no window, no Dock icon, and the app you're using keeps the focus.
- It's on by default. Satchel adds itself to your login items once, the first time this version runs from your Applications folder. It's listed in System Settings › General › Login Items.
- To turn it off, uncheck **Open Satchel at login** in Settings › General, or remove Satchel in System Settings › General › Login Items. Satchel never adds itself again after that. Settings always shows what macOS has.
- On macOS 11 and 12 the option is shown but can't be changed. You can add Satchel yourself in System Settings › Users & Groups › Login Items.

**Opening Satchel again shows the shelf**

- Opening Satchel while it's already running, from the Applications folder, Spotlight or Launchpad, now shows the shelf, just like the shortcut.

## Fixes

- **No more Satchel icon in the Dock.** Opening Satchel again while it was running put an icon in the Dock that did nothing when clicked and couldn't be closed there. Satchel now never appears in the Dock or the app switcher (⌘Tab), also not for a moment when it starts or after an update.
- **One Satchel at a time.** A second copy of Satchel (for example one opened from a downloaded disk image) now hands over to the one that's running and quits.

## Updating

- From 0.1.1 or 0.1.2: Satchel offers this version itself. Look for the dot on the menu bar icon, or choose **Check for Updates…** from its menu. After the update, Satchel adds itself to your login items once (see above).
- From 0.1.0: download the DMG below and drag Satchel to Applications, replacing the old one. Your history and settings stay as they are.

## Known limits

- macOS 11 or later on Apple silicon only. Opening at login needs macOS 13 or later.
- No sync between Macs.
